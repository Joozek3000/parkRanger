## Prototype

Time box: 6–8 weeks, networked from day one.
Success test: is it fun for 10 minutes with 2 people?

- One forest area, the road, and the town edge with the diner as a trigger zone
- 2 players with radios (range plus static)
- Tourists that wander, witness, take photos and flee to the diner
- Bigfoot, herded with food and a decoy
- Chores: feeding, trash
- Weirdness as a single number
- Afternoon-to-night lighting shift
- Stretch: one simple night threat (ghosts or shadow people) — default: shadow people
- Added by prototype spec draft (defaults, see `prototype-spec.md`): ranger station as spawn/return point; stop a fleeing tourist with a 2 s hold; Bigfoot hiding spot (cave); ignored chores draw Bigfoot

## Core (launch)

### Scope
- All zones plus vehicles; 4 players
- Full shift phases, seasons, Weirdness stages, inspector finale
- Chief ranger; FBI pair plus hotel sabotage
- Town: tool shop, diner, grocery
- Shift reports, fire tower and repeater, cosmetics

### Chores
- Maintenance
- Trash
- Lumberjacks
- Feeding
- Fines
- Fire watch
- Fishing

### Anomalies
- Bigfoot
- UFO
- Portals
- Ghosts
- Werewolves (night / full moon)
- Gnomes
- Sirens
- Baba Yaga
- Men in Black

### Equipment
- Feed, lures
- Inflatable Bigfoot decoy
- Tranquilizer
- Silver non-lethal tools (darts / nets / spray)
- Ghost traps
- Night vision
- Detection gadgets (EMF, UV, scanner)
- Camera
- Axe

### Minigames
- **Monsters:** Bigfoot herding, Baba Yaga chores, portal closing, werewolf sedation
- **Tourists:** tickets, phone confiscation, "it was a bear" talk, calming panic
- **Chores:** tree marking, trash sorting, repeater fixing, fire spotting

Open question (Q01): short in-shift interruptions (10–30 s) vs longer standalone activities. Likely a mix.
