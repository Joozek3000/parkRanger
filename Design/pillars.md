## Pitch

2–4 player online co-op party game in the vein of Peak / Lethal Company / Overcooked.
Players are park rangers keeping a park running (chores, tourists, animals, lumberjacks) while secretly hiding monsters and anomalies from the public.

## Pillars (settled)

1. **Chaos through communication**
   Radios, splitting up and bad coordination produce most of the fun.

2. **Keep the secret**
   Hiding the weird stuff is the core goal. Chores are the pressure that pulls players away from it.

3. **Funny, with teeth**
   Comedy by default. Scares are rare, earned, and followed by a laugh.

4. **A park with its own life**
   Tourists, animals, monsters and the FBI act on their own.

## Core rule (settled): manage, don't slay

- The chief ranger (NPC) says monsters are God's creatures and killing them is a sin.
- Lethal tools are not normal equipment.
- Killing can happen by accident, with a heavy penalty.
- Most problems are solved by working out what a creature wants.

## Using the pillars

When a new feature or idea comes in, note which pillar(s) it serves. If it serves none, flag it for discussion. It still gets logged in `ideas.md`.
